# Lingua Forge Academy PRO

A standalone professional English **and Italian** teaching workspace. Open `index.html` with Live Server (or any static server). No build step is required.

## English (EN)
Original, level-aligned curriculum: Kids Pre-A1–B2, Teens A1–B2, Adults A1–C2 — coursebooks, Grammar in Use (contents pages + numbered units, Murphy-style order), Vocabulary in Use (Oxford-style picture units), IELTS-style Reading Studio (paragraphs A–D with True/False/Not Given, Summary completion, Multiple choice), listening, speaking, writing, flashcards, workbooks, assessments, teacher tools, students, homework, planner (60/90-minute lesson programmes), games and progress.

- **1,155 vocabulary entries** (165+ per level, Pre-A1→C2) — every word with picture, IPA, part of speech, category, definition and example, organised as illustrated picture units in 6-part contents books. The complete vocabulary is also published as six **flip books** (Pre-A1→C2) with clickable contents.
- **29 grammar topics** (full English grammar coverage: be/have, articles, plurals, prepositions, all tenses including past continuous, past perfect, future perfect/continuous, quantifiers, determiners, comparatives, modals, conditionals, passive, reported speech, gerunds & infinitives, phrasal verbs, relative clauses, word formation, question tags, linking words, causatives, wishes & regrets, adverb clauses, advanced inversion/nominalisation) — every topic per CEFR level with a one-line rule, formula, use, examples, common error, extended study paragraph and checkable exercises. Grammar in Use is also a **flip grammar book** per level.
- **Coursebooks as flip books** — every coursebook (Kids/Teens/Adults, Pre-A1→C2) opens as a real flip book with cover, spine, contents and clickable lessons that jump straight into the lesson page.
- **Tips & Strategies** — 52 professional tips: IELTS exam tactics for all four papers, science-backed study methods (spaced repetition, active recall), lesson planning skeletons for 60/90-minute classes, and assessment/error-log strategies.
- **Activities & Games Studio** — six interactive, language-switchable games for EN and IT: Word Scramble, Hangman, Match It, Memory Pairs, Quiz Race and Sentence Builder, with TTS hints and three built-in level bands.

## Italiano (IT)
A full Italian section built like the classic series (Nuovo Espresso, Progetto Italiano, Grammatica in Uso):

- **Corsi sfogliabili (flip books)** — three coursebook series (A1–C2, 36 lessons) rendered as real flip books with a spine, turning pages, cover and clickable contents; each lesson has Lessico (illustrated), Grammatica (spiegazione + esercizi), Lettura (IELTS-style), Ascolto (TTS), Parlare and Scrivere.
- **Grammatica in Uso** — 25 grammar topics (A1–C2): comparativi, verbi modali, passato remoto, futuro anteriore, si impersonale, pronomi relativi, gerundio/participio, concordanza dei tempi and more — rule in one line + extended explanation + checkable exercises, available as an interactive flip grammar book per level.
- **Lessico in Uso** — 420 illustrated words (70 per level A1–C2) with picture, IPA and category, in 6 flip vocabulary books.
- **Lettura** — 8 authentic IELTS-style readings with Vero/Falso/Non indicato, summary completion and multiple choice, with Italian text-to-speech.
- **Ascolto (Listening Lab)** — authentic listening tracks with ▶ TTS playback per paragraph, transcripts, comprehension tasks and level filters.
- **Parlare (Speaking Studio)** — real conversation scenarios from every lesson with listen-to-the-task audio, checklists and a 1-minute speaking challenge timer.
- **Scrivere (Writing Studio)** — guided writing tasks per level with draft box, audio prompt and self-check checklist.
- **Risorse** — researched real resources: coursebooks (Nuovissimo Progetto Italiano, Nuovo Espresso, Chiaro!, Via del Corso, Al dente, Nuova grammatica pratica, Grammatica avanzata), YouTube channels (Learn Italian with Lucrezia, Italy Made Easy, Italiano Automatico, LearnAmo, Podcast Italiano) and websites (One World Italiano, Rai Scuola, Alma, Edilingua, Loescher, WordReference/Treccani).

Visuals use lightweight emoji/graphic illustrations and CSS so the app works offline without an image library.
